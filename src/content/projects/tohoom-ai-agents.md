As an AI Engineer at Tohoom LLC I own the development of AI agents in the company's Salesforce CIM product. The agents sit on top of Data Cloud and let users ask questions of their business data in plain language, then explain the figures that come back.

Before this role I spent a year as a Data Analyst at Tohoom, building **300–400 CRM Analytics dashboards** with SAQL and JSON for automotive-supplier customers. Sales teams and senior executives use them. I also wrote custom SAQL and SQL for sales presentations and fixed data-quality issues in Salesforce data recipes.
