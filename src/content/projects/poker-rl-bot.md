As Project Lead at the Michigan Data Science Team (MDST), I directed the development of a heads-up poker bot trained with deep reinforcement learning.

I designed a **pseudo-siamese architecture** and an **adapted clipped PPO loss** for the game, and ran sessions teaching the team the deep learning and RL fundamentals they needed to contribute.
