Through BU Spark! I led a student team working with a GBH News reporter on debt-collection lawsuits in Massachusetts.

The main obstacle was that the same collector appears under many different spellings. I designed an **NLP and regex-based matching system** that groups and standardizes those names. It improved case-identification accuracy by 33% and found more than 1,000 name variations no one had linked before. We presented the findings in an interactive **Tableau** dashboard with a heat map.
